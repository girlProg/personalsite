my sister had an old 32gb intel macbook with 1TB i just couldnt let go unused. not really old, the last of the line of intel macs, the 2019 16 inch. I started with wanting to install Ubuntu Server, so i can have a place to tinker and test out stuff. But because i had never done a partition and deleted the MacOS completely i was scared of turning it into a brick. Oh yes, forgot to mention the screen on the laptop was completely useless on the mac so everything i did depended on an external display. which is an art in itself learning how to make the external display take priority over the built in. Even considered making it [headless](https://www.xda-developers.com/i-turned-a-broken-macbook-headless/), but hardware itsnt my thing. maybe another time.

So i braced myself and took a plunge. I found a [YouTube video](https://youtu.be/YhNg-dSIA9k?si=iRJhY00BD7rxW6Nb) that detailed the entire process. It was perfect. The video followed instructions from [T2Linux](https://t2linux.org/). They advised installing ubuntu alongside the MacOS as a partition (for driver updates). Which was perfect for me cause i wasn't ready for the commitment. Only that there was a problem, i did not actually want Ubuntu Desktop, i wanted Ubuntu Server. I googled and it said they were close enough and i could still ssh and treat it like a server. So i did just that.

Coincidentally my husband was also working on setting up his homelab, with a bunch of raspberry pis and a lenovo machine. So i felt even more inspired to reach for Ubuntu Server. I kept looking, and i went down the YouTube rabbit hole of homelabs and discovered Proxmox. I couldn't believe something liken that existed. It was too good to be true. I could have many VMs of ubuntu server to play with and discard as i pleased? Count me in. I bing watched the process and i was ready. Same YouTuber [detailed the process](https://youtu.be/QT36YwXVVMk?si=__FAm6qc7Zq_4drR), and just like that, i had proxmox on my mac. I named it [argungu](https://www.google.com/search?q=argungu&rlz=1CDGOYI_enGB975GB975&oq=argungu&gs_lcrp=EgZjaHJvbWUyBggAEEUYOTIHCAEQLhiABDIGCAIQRRg8Mg0IAxAuGK8BGMcBGIAEMgcIBBAAGIAEMgcIBRAAGIAEMgkIBhAAGAoYgAQyCQgHEC4YChiABDIJCAgQLhgKGIAEMgcICRAAGIAE0gEIMTcyNWowajeoAhqwAgHiAwQYASBf8QUkl3rCkroVDvEFJJd6wpK6FQ4&hl=en-GB&sourceid=chrome-mobile&ie=UTF-8) (inspiration from husband) and spun a vn and installed ubuntu server on it. And i called that VM [bagaye](https://www.google.com/search?q=bagaye+kebbi&sca_esv=89447e19bfe2298c&rlz=1CDGOYI_enGB975GB975&hl=en-GB&sxsrf=ANbL-n5fFMdfUAhZ31IQPfca70Jgj6NSyw%3A1779686067780&ei=s9oTariuL6CzhbIP3a69sAk&biw=402&bih=684&oq=bagaye+kebbi&gs_lp=EhNtb2JpbGUtZ3dzLXdpei1zZXJwIgxiYWdheWUga2ViYmkyBRAhGKABMgUQIRigATIFECEYoAEyBRAhGKABSP4TUIgJWMIRcAB4AJABAZgBhQGgAYUHqgEDMi42uAEDyAEA-AEBmAIHoAKWBsICDRAAGIAEGLADGEMYigXCAgsQABiwAxgHGAoYHsICDBAAGIAEGLADGAoYC8ICEhAAGIAEGLADGLEDGIMBGAoYC8ICGxAuGIAEGLADGLEDGMcBGMgDGAoYCxivAdgBAcICHhAuGIAEGLADGLEDGIMBGMcBGMgDGAoYCxivAdgBAcICEhAuGIAEGLADGMgDGAoYC9gBAcICGBAuGIAEGLADGMcBGMgDGAoYCxivAdgBAcICCxAAGIAEGJECGIoFwgIHEAAYgAQYCsICBRAAGO8FwgIIEAAYFhgKGB7CAgcQABiABBgNwgIIEAAYBRgNGB7CAggQABgIGA0YHsICChAAGAgYChgNGB7CAgcQIRigARgKmAMAiAYBkAYRugYECAEYCJIHAzAuN6AHqE6yBwMwLje4B5YGwgcFMC4zLjTIBxSACAA&sclient=mobile-gws-wiz-serp), to keep the trend haha.
